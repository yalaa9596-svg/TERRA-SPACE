Slide 2 / 7 — The Problem

Why Farmers Lose Yield During Climate & Field Shifts

01

Guesswork Irrigation

Over-watering before unexpected rainfall wastes scarce water, while heat spikes dry root-zone moisture faster than traditional schedules adapt.

02

Crop Rotation Conflicts

Planting same-family crops consecutively (e.g., Wheat after Maize) depletes soil nutrients and compounds soil-borne pests.

03

Language & Expert Gap

Scientific dashboards are too complex for local growers, and farmers struggle to reach nearby specialists quickly when leaf disease strikes.

Slide 4 / 7 — Live Prototype Features

5-State NASA Irrigation Advisor & Crop Rotation Engine

Botanical Family & Climate Matcher

Combines 30-day T2M, RH2M, and GWETROOT against crop bounds. Flags family conflicts immediately (e.g., Poaceae → Poaceae) and suggests top climate-fit alternatives like Clover, Faba Bean, or Tomato.

Proactive Weather & Water Alerts

Translates raw satellite telemetry into simple liters-per-square-meter (L/m²) guidance and audible alerts in spoken Egyptian Arabic or English.

1. Urgent Irrigation
Dry root zone + heat > opt +3°C → Water immediately (~L/m²).
2. Irrigation Needed
Low soil moisture or dry heat → Schedule watering today.
3. Do Not Irrigate
≥5 mm rain forecasted in 48h → Skip watering & save water.
4. Heat / Cold Wave
Exceeds crop temp limits → Mulching & protective watering.
5. All Good
Soil wetness & temperature balanced → Field is healthy.

Slide 5 / 7 — Vision Doctor & Engineers

PlantDoc Disease Triage & Human-in-the-Loop Escalation

PlantDoc Vision & Safe Thresholding

  • Supports 27 disease & healthy classes across 11 PlantDoc crops using multimodal Gemini Vision and deterministic fallback.
  • Delivers observed symptoms, safe organic treatment, chemical recommendations, and spoken audio guidance.
  • Automatic Safety Escalation: Any diagnosis below 75% confidence, severe infection, or out-of-scope crop triggers immediate referral.

Geo-Matched Engineer Portal (Haversine)

  • Ranks agronomists by exact Haversine distance (km) and specialty (Plant Pathology, Field Crops, Vegetables, Fruits, Irrigation).
  • Farmers send consultations or tap direct call; escalated PlantDoc cases auto-appear in the engineer's queue.
  • Engineers inspect full T2M, GWETROOT, and PRECTOTCORR NASA telemetry before replying with prescriptions.
